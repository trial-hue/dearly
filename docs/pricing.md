Generated by `pnpm docs:pricing` from `src/domain/constants.ts` through `quote()`, `economics()` and the business and florist functions. Do not edit by hand: change the constants and regenerate. Consumer prices include VAT at 20% (ex VAT = total / 1.2); business prices exclude VAT. Money is integer pence inside the domain, rounded half up once at the end of each calculation.

## 4. Pick-up

- Offered only for Regular cards in Classic or Signature. Never Large, Giant or Luxe.
- The customer pays £1.95. No pick-up string says "free".
- Cost to Dearly: partner payout £1.75 + stock £0.25 = £2.00, which replaces the print cost. There is no delivery cost line and no guarantee reserve.
- Shown to the customer as "Ready today, within 2 hours", never "arrives".
- Moonpig comparison sentence: "Moonpig has no same-day physical card."

## 5. Delivery mode rule

Giant always travels tracked. Seven or more days ahead: advance post. Two to six days: tracked. Under two days: pick-up when rule 4 allows it, otherwise tracked with an on-the-day eCard offer. When the default would be pick-up but the finish is Luxe, tracked is used.

## 10. Guarantee

Advance and tracked only. A missed date gives a full refund and a single-use code for 50% off the card price (not delivery) of the next order, a tracked reprint when two or more days remain, and an on-the-day eCard always.

## 11. First card free

Once per account, for Regular Classic or Signature only, when the account holds at least 3 reminder dates and has not ordered a printed card before. The card price is 0; delivery, the digital copy and gifts are charged. Example: Regular Signature by advance post totals £0.95 (£0.79 ex VAT, contribution -£1.33).
