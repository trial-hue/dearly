# ADR 0005: Customers choose the date of a card

- Context: the storefront lets a customer pick who a card is for and when. The proposals service keyed proposals by person, occasion and year, and `createAdhocProposal` returned the existing proposal when the key already existed, silently dropping the date the customer chose. Nothing let a date be changed once a proposal existed, so the delivery rule (which mode and price follow from the days left) could not respond to the customer's date. The restructure brief froze the services, but a card the customer cannot date is not a working retailer.
- Decision: one additive change in `src/server/services/proposals.ts`: `setProposalDate(key, date, today)` moves a proposal's due date (and the ad hoc occasion's date when it has one), re-applies the delivery rule unless the customer overrode the mode, rejects past dates, and logs the move; `createAdhocProposal` now honours the requested date when the key already exists instead of returning the old date. The proposal endpoint gains one action, `set_date`. No schema, domain, price or rule changed.
- Alternatives: keying proposals by exact date (a schema and key-format change touching orders and the AI job); creating a second occasion type for "on a date" cards (confusing, and the reminder list would show duplicates); leaving the date fixed (rejected: the customer must be able to specify it).
- Consequences: the domain tests are unchanged; the service is covered end to end by the browse-to-order scenario, which now moves the date and checks that the mode and price follow; a proposal keeps its key when its date moves within the year.
- Date: 2026-09-20
