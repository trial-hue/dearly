# Drift: where the app disagreed with the business model

Each entry records a claim from `matrix.md` that failed when its test was first run, what the model expects, what the app did, the suspected cause, the severity, and the fix. Nothing here was resolved by weakening a test. Severity: **blocks the model** (a thesis claim does not hold), **misleads a customer** (a customer would see or pay something wrong), **cosmetic** (wording or a label).
